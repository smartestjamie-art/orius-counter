Stitched pod

      
Each side's shots joined where they overlap: every part of the pod appears once, so
        every egg is counted once. If a join shows a step or a repeated piece, drag its Overlap (and Up / down) until the
        pod runs on smoothly and press Save for these shots: the stage steps the same way each time, so it holds for every pod, and
        later pods are matched close to it.
