Overlap between rail marks

Shots at neighbouring marks of the same side overlap. Each overlap is split down the middle, so every egg is counted once; shaded parts are not counted.

Every clear match is remembered for its pair of marks, and used where a pair cannot be matched, such as a short pod with only plain card in the overlap.
