Scan

The stage needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (not Safari, Firefox, a phone or a tablet).

Not connected

1. Plug in the USB and switch on the 12 V. 2. Press Use camera (top right). 3. Press Connect stage (the first time on a computer, pick the Arduino's port); it finds its switches by itself in about 40 s.

Per pod: lay it along the rails, its right end under the lens, press Scan this side; it runs to the pod's other end on its own. Turn the pod over, press Side B next, scan again, save, then Next pod. Photos stay in this browser until saved.

Adjust by hand

Download focus helper for Mac

Jog: click for one step, hold to keep moving. Keys: ← → X, ↑ ↓ Y, Page Up/Down Z. Every move stays inside the rig's limits.

Rig settings

Measured on this rig and the same on every computer: change only when the rig itself changes.
