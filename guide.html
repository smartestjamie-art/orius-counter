Pod Scanner Guide

Photographing whole bean pods, sharp from end to end, with the motorised microscope rig. One side takes about 5 minutes.

Page: smartestjamie-art.github.io/orius-counter/eggs.html
Use Chrome or Edge (on Mac or Windows). Safari and Firefox cannot talk to the rig.

1. Start of the day

  1. Plug the rig's USB cable and the microscope's USB cable into the computer. Switch on the power supply (24 V) last.
  2. Open the page in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Press Use camera (top right). The live microscope picture appears.
  4. Press Connect stage. The first time on a computer, pick the Arduino from the list.
  5. Wait about 40 s: the rig finds its three limit switches by itself (Z up, then Y, then X) and parks at the start spot (X 1, Y 16, Z −29). The status line then ends in homed.
  6. If instead it says where the stage is is not known: press each of the three limit switches by hand. Each press should light the switch's small LED and show switch X, switch Y or switch Z on the status line. Only then press Home (under Adjust by hand). If a switch shows nothing, fix its plug first: homing without it can drive that slide into its end.

2. Scanning one pod

  1. Type the pod's name in Pod ID (for example Pod 12).
  2. Lay the pod along the rails, its right end under the lens (the stage is parked at the start spot).
  3. Fine-tune with the keyboard: arrow keys move X and Y, Page Up / Page Down move Z (the jog step is set under Adjust by hand). Stop when the pod's right end is in the picture, the pod runs across the middle, and its surface is sharp.
  4. Press Scan this side. First it runs along the pod once without shooting (about 30 s) to check that Y can keep the whole pod in the picture all the way; if it cannot, it shoots nothing and says which way to move the pod. Then it shoots from exactly where you left the stage: X steps about 26 mm per shot, Y follows the pod (a shot whose pod runs off the top or bottom is taken again), Z sweeps from 8 mm below to 10 mm above the focus you set, and it stops by itself at the pod's tail. The stitched pod grows underneath the live picture.
  5. If a box asks whether to start this side again: OK starts it again from where the stage is (the side's old shots are removed); Cancel carries on after them.
  6. When it says Flip the pod: turn the pod over, fine-tune again (right end in the picture, across the middle, sharp), press Side B next, then Scan this side.
  7. If it says Not started or that the pod lies too far forward (or back): move the pod the way it says (or lay it straighter along the rails), fine-tune again and press Scan this side. The whole pod must be in every picture, or the stitching cannot join it.
  8. Press Next pod. This saves the pod first (one .zip in your Downloads folder), then moves on to the next pod's name.

To change where Home parks (for example after the microscope was moved), fine-tune to the new place and press Remember this spot under Adjust by hand.

Egg numbers: the page counts round white spots and some are not eggs; the true number is about 0.75 × the page's number. Check eggs on this pod (under the stitched picture) gives a corrected number in about a minute.

3. Saving

  • Each pod is saved as one file: Pod_12_YYYYMMDD.zip, in your Downloads folder. It holds every shot as taken plus the stitched picture of each side.
  • Next pod saves automatically. Save this pod's photos saves at any time.
  • The line under the buttons says whether the pod is saved yet.
  • Copy the day's .zip files to the shared drive before you leave. Photos also stay in this browser, but only on this computer.

4. If something goes wrong

Something is about to hit, or the rig makes a bad noisePress Stop. Everything stops at once. Then press Home (under Adjust by hand) before scanning again.
“A limit signal flickered…”Nothing to do: it finds its switches again and carries on with the scan by itself.
“A limit switch was hit: backing off…”It moves off the switch by itself. Check nothing is in the way, then press Home and scan again. The scan carries on from the missing shots.
The live picture is black or frozenPress Use camera again. If the wrong camera shows, choose Digital Microscope under Adjust by hand.
“Not connected” / Connect does nothing / “The device has been lost”Check the USB cable at both ends and the power supply, reload the page, press Connect stage again. Close any other tab that has the page open (only one page can hold the rig). If the USB keeps dropping when the motors start, use a short USB cable or a powered USB hub.
“… went its whole travel without its switch answering”Stop. Do not press Home again until that switch is checked: press it by hand and see switch X/Y/Z on the status line.
“Some parts are sharpest at the top/bottom of the band: adding…” or “sweeping again slower…”Nothing to do: the shot is being checked and topped up until every part of the pod is sharp. Keep the scanner page in front during a scan (a hidden page gets no pictures from the camera).
Pressing Home during a scanIt does nothing while a scan runs. Press Stop first, then Home: it finds the switches and parks at the start spot. It does not restart the scan; press Scan this side to go on from the next shot.
The scan stopped early, before the tailPress Scan this side again: it continues from the next shot.
The pictures are blurred everywhereThe microscope's height may have changed (rig moved or knocked). Tell Jamie: the focus band needs measuring again.

5. Please don't

  • Don't turn the microscope's own focus or zoom ring. It is set and taped.
  • Don't push the slides by hand while the power is on, and don't put anything behind the tray (the column is at the back).
  • Don't change anything under Rig settings.
  • Don't open the page in two tabs at once.

6. End of the day

  1. Make sure the last pod is saved (press Save this pod's photos if the line says it is not).
  2. Copy the .zip files to the shared drive.
  3. Switch off the 24 V supply, then unplug the USB.
